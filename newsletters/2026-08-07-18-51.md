## Headlines

1. **Off day at Halas Hall — triage continues behind closed doors**
   After camp’s longest, most physical session Thursday (~2:10), the Bears took Friday off as published. Sports Illustrated’s Gene Chamberlain noted the club may not issue injury clarity until **Saturday or Sunday** because of the rest day — so the first real “who’s back?” look comes when pads (or shells) hit again Saturday morning. That open practice is also **Celebrating Women and Girls in Sports Day** (gates 7:45 a.m., practice 8:30 a.m. CT). Defensive coordinator **Dennis Allen** remains expected to miss Saturday’s session for **Drew Brees’s Hall of Fame induction** in Canton.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Day 8 / next](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/06/bears-training-camp-report-day-8-qb-caleb-williams-sharper-bryson-graham-nfl-bulls), [SI — Johnson’s physical practices](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/ben-johnson-loves-his-tough-practices-but-are-there-limitations), [chicagobears.com — camp schedule](https://www.chicagobears.com/fan-zone/training-camp)

2. **Injury board holds: Jones optimism, everyone else still foggy**
   Nothing material changed Friday on the five players who left Day 8 early. **Braxton Jones** (LT) still carries the best early vibes — Brad Biggs and Courtney Cronin framed it as dodging a significant injury / likely **day-to-day** after more evaluation. **Montez Sweat** walked off under his own power after slowing in 11-on-11, but severity remains **unknown**. Depth DBs **Owens** (carted) and **Flowers** (helped off) looked the worst Thursday; **Jaylon Jones** missing late periods pushed rookie **Malik Muhammad** into first-team nickel. Until Saturday’s attendance chart, treat Thursday’s early reports as the latest confirmed word.
   **Sources:** [Sun-Times — Jones/Sweat](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/06/bears-training-camp-braxton-jones-leaves-practice-injury-ben-johnson-left-tackle), [Bears Wire — Jones update](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/06/bears-braxton-jones-avoids-significant-injury/91201016007/), [SI — Day 8](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-bears-training-camp-day-8-an-injury-filled-practice)

3. **Friday stock check: rookies under the microscope**
   With eight practices in the books, Patrick Finley’s Sun-Times rookie superlatives are the clearest *new* Bears feature of the off day. **Dillon Thieneman** draws “most pressure” with **Coby Bryant** out — Johnson said Tuesday’s film finally showed something closer to Thieneman’s college play speed, even if the rookie is still thinking too much. **Jordan van den Berg** is the “biggest surprise,” including **first-team run-game snaps** Thursday. **Zavion Thomas** is Johnson’s versatile chess piece (outside, slot, maybe RB); **Logan Jones** remains the 2027-centered plan behind **Garrett Bradbury**; **Muhammad** is learning nickel by necessity; **Keyshaun Elliott** stays an incomplete grade on **PUP** (back); **Sam Roush** earns the “stud with pads on” praise from Loveland and Kmet.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Rating the rookies](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/07/bears-training-camp-rookies-draft-pick-rate-superlatives-nfl-dillon-thieneman-zavion-thomas-malik-muhammad-jodan-van-den-berg)

4. **Week 1 winners/losers: Davis, van den Berg, Ahmed flash; Gordon and Allen feel the secondary squeeze**
   Bears Wire’s Friday recap of the last four practices crowns **Kaden Davis** (one-handed Wednesday TD, another score Thursday from Keenum), **van den Berg**, and **Salvon Ahmed** (pushing for RB3) as winners, with **Jones** a cautious “winner if healthy.” Losers: **Bryant**, **Gordon** (still rehabbing, no update), and a defense/Allen unit thinned by secondary attrition. It’s analysis more than news — but it matches what beat notes have been building all week.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire / Yahoo — winners & losers](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/winners-losers-chicago-bears-first-133421684.html)

5. **Rumor / unconfirmed: Sweat scare rekindles Maxx Crosby chatter**
   After Sweat’s early exit, some outlets floated Chicago revisiting a **Maxx Crosby** trade. Separate Schefter podcast comments (midweek) sketched a scenario where Crosby could resurface near the **November deadline** if the Raiders struggle — and nodded when the Bears were named as a possible landing spot. That is **not** reporting of active talks tied to Thursday’s practice. Label it speculative unless Poles or Schefter confirm movement.
   **Sources:** [SI — Schefter Crosby hypothetical](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/adam-schefter-ignites-maxx-crosby-to-chicago-bears-trade-buzz), [Heavy — Crosby chatter after Sweat](https://heavy.com/sports/nfl/chicago-bears/maxx-crosby-trade-montez-sweat-injury/)
