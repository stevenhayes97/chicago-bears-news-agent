# Chicago Bears Brief — 2026-08-04

**Estimated read:** 5 min · **Window:** last 1 day

## TL;DR
- **Coby Bryant** is expected out **8–10 weeks** (hyperextended left knee, bone bruise, small fracture); **no surgery** projected. He posted thanks for the support Tuesday and vowed to return stronger.
- **Tuesday** was the **first open padded practice** of camp — **Day 6** overall — with live periods and a **defense-heavy** morning while the **offense sputtered**.
- **Cam Lewis** is working at **starting safety** opposite **Dillon Thieneman** while **Kyler Gordon** remains on **PUP**; Dennis Allen said Lewis will focus on safety for now.
- **Luther Burden III** took a hard **goal-line hit** from **Devin Bush** in a two-minute drill and was slow to get up but walked off and was moving on the sideline; worth monitoring.
- **Kyle Monangai** practiced for a **second straight day** after his soft-tissue absence; **Terell Smith** and **KC Eziomume** were not spotted Tuesday.

## Headlines

1. **Bryant timeline firms up: 8–10 weeks, no surgery — secondary in crisis mode**
   The worst news from Monday’s first padded session got clearer overnight and into Tuesday: ESPN’s Adam Schefter reported that new safety **Coby Bryant** hyperextended his left knee, suffered a **bone bruise and small fracture**, and is expected to miss **eight to 10 weeks** without needing surgery. The Chicago Sun-Times, citing a source, framed the calendar bluntly — Bryant could miss **at least the first three games** (Panthers, Vikings, Eagles) and might not be back until the **Week 5** trip to Green Bay on the long end of the range. Bryant was hurt covering **Cole Kmet** in a 1-on-1; he was carted off but under his own power and was visibly upset. On Tuesday morning he addressed fans on social media: *“Thank you for all the prayers & love! God makes no mistakes & I'll be back stronger.”* With **Elijah Hicks** on PUP and **Kyler Gordon** still out, the Bears’ offseason investment in the back end — Bryant’s three-year, $40 million deal plus first-round pick Thieneman — is being stress-tested before the regular season even starts.
   **Sources:** [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/49522578/new-bears-safety-coby-bryant-8-10-weeks-injury), [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/03/bears-s-coby-bryant-carted-off-from-practice-with-apparent-knee-injury), [Bears Wire](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/04/bears-coby-bryant-reacts-knee-injury/91162706007/)

2. **Defense wins open padded day; offense answers with a flat performance**
   Fans returned to Halas Hall on **Tuesday, Aug. 4**, for the second straight padded practice and the camp’s **first public** padded session. Beat reporters described a **sloppier day for the offense** and a defense that fed off the added intensity — including the summer’s **first scuffle**, when sixth-round DT **Jordan van den Berg** slammed UDFA OL **Caden Barnett** to the turf during third-team work (Yahoo Sports). Montez Sweat and the defensive front were “fired up,” per observers. That flip follows a camp narrative where the passing game had largely controlled non-padded days; padded live work is exposing both sides. **Garrett Bradbury** continued to run with the first team at center, and **Braxton Jones** warmed up with the ones at left tackle as the competition with **Jedrick Wills Jr.** and **Kiran Amegadjie** rolls on.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire — live blog](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/04/bears-training-camp-live-updates-news-second-padded-practice--live/91160611007/), [Yahoo Sports — scuffle](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/first-scuffle-bears-training-camp-154507430.html)

3. **Burden scare in two-minute drill; Johnson keeps feeding the Year 2 hype**
   Late in Tuesday’s practice, **Luther Burden III** was **hit from behind** by linebacker **Devin Bush** on a fourth-down pass near the goal line in a **two-minute** period. Tribune beat reporter Brad Biggs noted Burden was **slow to get up** but walked off on his own; Bear Report’s Zack Pearson saw him moving on the sideline afterward. No official injury designation followed by mid-day, but it’s the kind of hit that raises eyebrows on a day the team can’t afford more skill-player setbacks. Earlier in the window, Ben Johnson told Kay Adams the **“Luther Burden hype is real”** — expanding his route tree, using him inside and outside, and expecting more from his run blocking now that pads are on. Monday’s padded work already featured Burden winning a contested rep against **Tyrique Stevenson** down the sideline.
   **Sources:** [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/luther-burden-injury-watch-bears-big-hit-training-camp), [Bears Wire — Johnson on Burden](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/04/bears-ben-johnson-hype-surrounding-luther-burden/91152145007/)

4. **Allen locks Lewis at safety; nickel picture stays murky without Gordon**
   Before Tuesday’s practice, defensive coordinator **Dennis Allen** told reporters that with Bryant sidelined, **Cam Lewis** will **focus on safety** — the role he filled opposite Thieneman in 11-on-11 on Monday after Bryant left. Lewis had been taking nickel reps with Gordon on PUP, which opens the slot to **Jaylon Jones**, rookie **Malik Muhammad**, and **Josh Blackwell** in the rotation beat writers are tracking. The Sun-Times and Windy City Gridiron both flagged how thin the room looks: beyond Lewis and Thieneman, the remaining safeties on the roster have **minimal NFL experience**. Jaylon Johnson’s health and Thieneman’s rapid development just became central to the 2026 defensive outlook.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire — Bryant reaction / Allen](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/04/bears-coby-bryant-reacts-knee-injury/91162706007/), [Windy City Gridiron](https://www.windycitygridiron.com/chicago-bears-coaching-staff/120007/injuries-put-more-pressure-dennis-allen-bears-defense-to-simply-survive-in-2026)

5. **Monangai ramps up; Smith still missing from the field**
   **Kyle Monangai** was on the field for a **second consecutive day** after missing the first three practices with a soft-tissue issue Ben Johnson has described as **day-to-day** and likely on the shorter side of the recovery spectrum. He had been limited in 11-on-11 earlier in the week; Tuesday continued the ramp-up behind **D’Andre Swift**. At corner, **Terell Smith** — who left Monday’s padded practice after appearing to limp following a rep against **Zavion Thomas** — was **not spotted** Tuesday, along with **KC Eziomume**, per Bears Wire’s live updates. The team has not always issued same-day clarity on camp absences, but the secondary cannot afford a pile-on after Bryant’s news.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire — live blog](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/04/bears-training-camp-live-updates-news-second-padded-practice--live/91160611007/), [Chicago Sun-Times — Monday report](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/03/bears-training-camp-report-practice-no-5-rb-kyle-monangai-back-in-action)
