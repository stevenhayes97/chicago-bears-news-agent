# Chicago Bears Brief — 2026-08-05

**Estimated read:** 4 min · **Window:** last 1 day

## TL;DR
- **Day 7** at Halas Hall was a **shells-only, abbreviated** session in light rain — a breather after two padded practices, with limited 11-on-11 to read into.
- **T.J. Edwards** was **activated off the NFI list** and returned to practice after missing the first six sessions with an offseason **calf strain**; the Bears will ramp him up gradually.
- **Caleb Williams** and the offense **bounced back** in 7-on-7: beat writers had him **9-of-10** with **five touchdowns**, capped by a diving one-handed score from **Kaden Davis**.
- **Terell Smith** missed a **second straight** practice; **KC Eziomume** and **Jordan McFadden** were also absent. **Joe Thuney** and **Grady Jarrett** sat as **veteran rest**, not injury.
- **Coby Bryant** timeline unchanged (8–10 weeks); no new team update today. Secondary depth remains the camp’s dominant storyline.

## Headlines

1. **Edwards is back — run defense gets its veteran anchor**
   Two days after the secondary absorbed Coby Bryant’s long-term knee news, the Bears got a meaningful defensive reprieve on Wednesday. The club **activated linebacker T.J. Edwards from the Non-Football Injury list** before practice; he had strained his calf while training away from the facility over the summer, missing the camp’s first six sessions. Ryan Poles had downplayed the issue last week, and Edwards — who broke his leg in January’s playoff win over Green Bay but has recovered from that — was on the field for Day 7. The Chicago Sun-Times noted the Bears will likely **ease him in** on conditioning after the layoff. Edwards has been one of Chicago’s most productive defenders when healthy (351 tackles in 44 starts over his first four Bears seasons) and pairs with newcomer **Devin Bush** in a unit trying to fix a run defense that ranked **sixth-worst** in the NFL last year.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/05/bears-lb-t-j-edwards-returns-to-practice-after-missing-first-week-with-calf-injury), [Sports Illustrated — Day 7 roundup](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-day-7-of-chicago-bears-training-camp)

2. **Offense answers Tuesday’s padded slog with a sharp 7-on-7 day**
   After beat writers tagged the offense with three consecutive uneven practices — including Tuesday’s “sloppiest” padded showing — **Caleb Williams** flipped the script on a lighter Wednesday. In shells, with no pads and a shortened practice, the first-team offense dominated 7-on-7. Bear Report’s Zack Pearson reported Williams went **9-for-10** with **five touchdown passes**; Adam Hoge of CHGO noted the only incompletion was a **throwaway**. Scores went to **Kyle Monangai**, **Luther Burden III**, **Colston Loveland** (twice), and **Kaden Davis**, who made what Pearson called the **“catch of camp so far”** — a diving **one-handed** grab in the end zone. Sports Illustrated cautioned not to over-read a pass-heavy, non-padded day, especially with the defense having won the prior three sessions. Still, for a group that lives on rhythm and timing, Wednesday was the reset Ben Johnson’s unit needed before pads return Thursday.
   **Sources:** [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-day-7-of-chicago-bears-training-camp), [Bears Wire — Day 7 live hub](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/05/bears-training-camp-live-updates-news-day-7--live/91175839007/)

3. **Position battles: Jones at left tackle, Booker pushing at edge**
   Brad Biggs’s Wednesday notebook framed a few competitions as settling or sharpening. **Braxton Jones** appears to be **locking in as the starting left tackle**, the role many expected entering camp. On the other side of the line, **second-year edge Austin Booker** is making a **strong push to start opposite Montez Sweat** over **Dayo Odeyingbo**, per the Sun-Times. That battle matters for a defense that did not add a marquee pass rusher this offseason and finished **tied for seventh-fewest sacks** in 2025. With Bryant still out, **Cam Lewis** continues at **starting safety** opposite **Dillon Thieneman** while **Kyler Gordon** remains on **PUP** — Dennis Allen said earlier this week Lewis will focus on safety for now, reopening the nickel rotation for **Jaylon Jones**, **Malik Muhammad**, and **Josh Blackwell**.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Day 7 report](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/05/bears-lb-t-j-edwards-returns-to-practice-after-missing-first-week-with-calf-injury), [Chicago Sun-Times — Bryant cascade](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/04/bears-training-camp-coby-bryant-dennis-allen-safety-injury-knee-cascading-effect-problems-solve-nfl)

4. **Smith still sidelined; Burden clear after Tuesday’s goal-line hit**
   The injury board did not worsen Wednesday, but it did not clear up either. **Terell Smith** was **not at practice** for a second consecutive day after leaving Monday’s padded session, joining **KC Eziomume** and **Jordan McFadden** among the absences Brad Biggs tracked. That keeps pressure on a secondary already without Bryant, Gordon (PUP), and Elijah Hicks (PUP). On a positive note, **Luther Burden III** — slow to get up after **Devin Bush** broke up a goal-line pass Tuesday — **practiced normally** and was among Williams’s touchdown targets Wednesday. Burden told the Sun-Times on Tuesday he expects to **“catch every ball”** and is targeting an **“explosive”** Year 2; today’s rep chart matched that confidence.
   **Sources:** [Sports Illustrated — Day 7](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-day-7-of-chicago-bears-training-camp), [Chicago Sun-Times — Tuesday practice report](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/04/bears-practice-report-caleb-williams-luther-burden-devin-bush-nfl-goal-line-ryan-poles-halas-hall-tuesday)

5. **Internal plan at safety: Sewell signing, no trade hunt (for now)**
   No new Bryant news today — ESPN’s **8–10 week** timeline and **no surgery** expectation from Monday still stand, and Bryant’s social-media thank-you from Tuesday is the latest word from the player. The roster response remains **internal**: Tuesday’s signing of UDFA safety **Marlen Sewell** (with **Jonathan Garvin** to IR) read to Biggs as a signal Chicago is **not shopping for external safety help** yet. That aligns with leaning on Lewis, Thieneman, and developmental pieces rather than burning draft capital mid-August — though camp has a long way to go before Week 1 at Carolina.
   **Sources:** [ESPN — Bryant timeline](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/49522578/new-bears-safety-coby-bryant-8-10-weeks-injury), [Yahoo Sports — Biggs on Sewell](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/bears-roster-moves-send-eye-172002302.html)
