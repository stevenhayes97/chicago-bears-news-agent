# Chicago Bears Brief — 2026-08-08

**Estimated read:** 5 min · **Window:** last 1 days

## TL;DR
- **Day 9** made the secondary crisis worse: **Cam Lewis** — Bryant’s fill-in at safety — was **carted off** late in practice (apparent right ankle/foot / leg-hip pain).
- **Luther Burden** limped off ~45 minutes in after an awkward 1-on-1 tangle with **Tyrique Stevenson**; Stevenson stayed, Burden left with a trainer.
- Roster churn: Friday — **Xavier Woods** signed, **Dallis Flowers** to IR; Saturday — **Brandon Hill** and **William Wright** signed, **Gervarrius Owens** and **K.C. Eziomume** waived with injury designations.
- **Braxton Jones** and **Montez Sweat** sat out Saturday; sources/Sun-Times say **neither has a serious injury**. Amegadjie and Wills rotated at LT and struggled; rookie **Malik Muhammad** was solid in the slot.
- Continuity vs. Thursday’s brief: the Day 8 injury pile-on is now a full secondary rebuild mid-camp — Woods/Hill are the immediate patch while Lewis’s status is the next watch item.

## Headlines

1. **Cam Lewis carted off — the safety depth chart collapses again**
   After Friday’s off day, Saturday’s open practice (Celebrating Women and Girls in Sports Day) delivered the worst possible sequel to Thursday’s triage session. Lewis, shifted from nickel to safety after **Coby Bryant**’s knee injury, got hurt on the second-to-last play of an end-of-game period, limped to the sideline, stayed down, and left on a cart (ESPN’s Courtney Cronin). Brad Biggs framed it as a right ankle/foot look; Patrick Finley’s Sun-Times report described considerable pain as Lewis tried to stretch his leg and hip. With Bryant still expected out for weeks, **Elijah Hicks** on PUP, and Owens waived Saturday after Thursday’s cart-off, Chicago’s safety room is now veteran **Xavier Woods**, rookie **Dillon Thieneman**, new addition **Brandon Hill**, and developmental pieces — a far cry from the Bryant–Thieneman plan entering camp.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Day 9](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/08/bears-training-camp-report-day-9-safety-injuries-cam-lewis-jason-garrett-colston-loveland), [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/coby-bryant-replacement-cam-lewis-carted-bears-practice-injury)

2. **Burden exits early — Year 2 breakout day ends with a limp**
   About 45 minutes into practice, Burden and Stevenson landed awkwardly on the last 1-on-1 rep in the end zone. Stevenson stretched his hip/groin and continued; Burden took a knee, walked with a limp, and followed trainer Andre Tucker inside (Sun-Times). Burden has been one of camp’s brightest offensive storylines — including Thursday’s two-minute TD from Caleb Williams — and Johnson has publicly hyped his trajectory. No severity update yet; Sunday’s practice will be the first checkpoint.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Burden](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/08/bears-training-camp-luther-burden-leaves-practice-early-with-injury), [Bears Wire / Yahoo](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/bears-wr-luther-burden-exits-143638915.html)

3. **Poles restocks the secondary: Woods Friday, Hill & Wright Saturday**
   On the off day, Chicago signed nine-year safety **Xavier Woods** (one-year deal) and placed **Dallis Flowers** on injured reserve after Thursday’s right-leg injury — effectively ending Flowers’ season. Saturday morning brought two more DBs: safety **Brandon Hill** (ex-Texans seventh-rounder, mostly special teams) and CB **William Wright** (Tennessee UDFA), while **Owens** and **Eziomume** were waived with injury designations. Woods’ 121 career starts and Al Harris reunion give the Bears a real emergency starter option opposite Thieneman if Lewis is out; Hill is depth insurance, not a splash fix.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Woods](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/07/bears-training-camp-xavier-woods-veteran-safety-sign-free-agent-nfl), [chicagobears.com — Aug. 7 moves](https://www.chicagobears.com/news/chicago-bears-announce-roster-moves-august-7-2026), [Windy City Gridiron](https://www.windycitygridiron.com/chicago-bears-news/120237/bears-add-two-dbs-waive-two-others)

4. **Jones & Sweat miss Day 9 — early word still encouraging**
   Both projected starters sat Saturday after Thursday’s early exits. Finley reported neither has a serious injury, echoing Friday sourcing that Jones avoided long-term damage. In their absence, **Kiran Amegadjie** and **Jedrick Wills** rotated at left tackle and struggled; on defense, slot snaps went to rookie **Malik Muhammad**, who drew positive notes. The LT clarity Johnson wanted by mid-next week is on hold until Jones returns, but the baseline remains day-to-day optimism rather than a season-altering blow.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Day 9](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/08/bears-training-camp-report-day-9-safety-injuries-cam-lewis-jason-garrett-colston-loveland), [Chicago Sun-Times — Burden/Jones-Sweat note](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/08/bears-training-camp-luther-burden-leaves-practice-early-with-injury)

5. **Loveland’s TD celebration, empty coaches’ box, thin bright spots**
   Away from the carts: **Colston Loveland** hauled in a spectacular 7-on-7 touchdown, then punted the ball in celebration and drew a practice flag — the day’s viral moment (Finley). Defensive coordinator **Dennis Allen** and OL coach **Dan Roushar** missed practice for **Drew Brees**’s Hall of Fame induction in Canton. Also sitting: CBs **Terell Smith** and **Jaylon Jones**, plus LB **D’Marco Jackson**. For a unit already patching Bryant and Gordon holes, Saturday was about surviving the session and seeing who can practice Sunday.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Day 9](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/08/bears-training-camp-report-day-9-safety-injuries-cam-lewis-jason-garrett-colston-loveland)
