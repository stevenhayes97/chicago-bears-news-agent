# Chicago Bears Brief — 2026-08-03

**Estimated read:** 4 min · **Window:** last 1 day

## TL;DR
- **First padded practice** at Halas Hall on Monday — closed to the public (invitation-only day on the camp calendar).
- **Coby Bryant** was **carted off** after landing awkwardly in a 1-on-1 drill; he was grabbing his **left knee**. No official diagnosis from the team yet.
- **Kyle Monangai** returned in **full pads** after a soft-tissue absence; Ben Johnson said he’s **day-to-day** and the team expects a shorter recovery.
- **Ben Johnson** compared **Luther Burden III** to **Amon-Ra St. Brown**, highlighting Burden’s work ethic and Year 2 expectations after the DJ Moore departure.
- Teammates (including **Cole Kmet**) praised **Garrett Bradbury’s** communication at center as a smooth handoff from retired Drew Dalman.

## Headlines

1. **Coby Bryant exits first padded practice on a cart**
   Chicago’s marquee offseason safety signing left Monday’s session early after an awkward landing during a 1-on-1 rep against Cole Kmet. Reporters on site said Bryant was holding his left knee and appeared emotional as he was carted to the locker room. Bryant was expected to anchor one starting safety spot alongside rookie Dillon Thieneman after Chicago lost Kevin Byard, Jaquan Brisker, and Tremaine Edmunds in free agency — so any significant absence would sting in a secondary already waiting on Kyler Gordon.
   **Sources:** [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/coby-bryant-carted-chicago-bears-practice-leg-injury), [Yahoo Sports (live blog)](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/live-updates-first-padded-practice-140611459.html)

2. **Kyle Monangai back on the field in full pads**
   Monangai had missed three straight practices without a public update before Johnson clarified a soft-tissue issue and a day-to-day plan. Monangai suited up for Monday’s padded work and took part in warmups — a relief for depth behind D’Andre Swift after Salvon Ahmed got extended reps last week. Johnson framed the goal as keeping Monangai available for a 17-plus-game season rather than rushing him through camp.
   **Sources:** [A to Z Sports](https://atozsports.com/nfl/chicago-bears-news/bears-receive-much-needed-update-on-kyle-monangai-injury-training-camp-ben-johnson/), [Yahoo Sports (live blog)](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/news/live-updates-first-padded-practice-140611459.html)

3. **Johnson: Luther Burden has a “desire to be great” like Amon-Ra St. Brown**
   In a FOX 32 interview published Monday morning, Johnson tied Burden’s competitive edge and conditioning focus to the traits he saw in elite receivers he coached in Miami and Detroit. The praise lands as Burden enters a larger role after the Bears moved on from DJ Moore; he finished his rookie year with 652 yards and two touchdowns, with most of that production coming in the second half of the season.
   **Sources:** [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/bears-ben-johnson-luther-burden-amon-ra-st-brown-desire-to-be-great)

4. **Garrett Bradbury earning trust in the huddle**
   With Drew Dalman’s retirement forcing a quick trade for Bradbury, Kmet said communication has been “flawless” through walkthroughs and non-padded practices — important for Caleb Williams, who is on his third center in three years. Williams had noted a small miscommunication early in camp; padded practices are the next test for timing and pass-pro clarity.
   **Sources:** [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/bears-teammates-highlight-garrett-bradburys-010439723.html)

5. **Pads on, fans mostly off — camp calendar note**
   Monday marked the fifth practice of camp and the first in pads. Bears Wire noted the session was closed to the public; the Tribune’s camp schedule also lists invitation-only practices today and Aug. 11, with the next **open** Halas Hall practice on **Tuesday, Aug. 4**.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/02/bears-training-camp-recap-day-4/91137050007/), [Chicago Tribune](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2026/06/30/bears-training-camp-schedule-lake-forest/)

## Roster & injuries
- **Coby Bryant (S):** Left practice on a cart Monday; severity **unknown** — monitor team reports.
- **Kyle Monangai (RB):** Soft tissue; **day-to-day**, practiced in full pads Monday.
- **Kyler Gordon (CB):** Still on **PUP** with a calf issue; Ryan Poles and Johnson expressed frustration with the timeline when camp opened (no new return date reported today).
- **T.J. Edwards (LB):** **NFI** list with a calf injury suffered away from Halas Hall; Poles has downplayed it as non-major.
- **Montez Sweat (DL):** Activated from the non-football illness list when camp opened; illness had sidelined him early in camp per ESPN’s rolling camp file.
