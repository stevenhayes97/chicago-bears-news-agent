# Chicago Bears Brief — 2026-08-03

**Estimated read:** 5 min · **Window:** last 1 day

## TL;DR
- **Day 5 / first padded practice** wrapped around **10:17 a.m. CT** at Halas Hall (invitation-only; not a public open practice).
- **Coby Bryant** was **carted off** after an awkward landing in a **1-on-1 vs. Cole Kmet**, grabbing his **left knee**; **no official diagnosis** yet. **Cam Lewis** took his first-team reps; Ryan Poles and Kevin Warren were seen getting an update on the field.
- **Kyle Monangai** returned in **full pads** after three missed practices; Ben Johnson called it a **soft-tissue** issue and **day-to-day**, expecting a **shorter** absence.
- **Braxton Jones** was back with the **first-team offense at left tackle** as the rotation continues; **Jaylon Jones** got looks at **nickel**.
- **Terell Smith** also **left early** with trainers late in practice — nature of the issue **unknown**.

## Headlines

1. **Coby Bryant carted off in the scariest moment of camp so far**
   Monday’s padded work turned on a non-contact rep: Bryant landed awkwardly covering Kmet in 1-on-1s, grabbed his left knee, slammed his helmet, and left on a cart (still under his own power). Beat reporters including Adam Hoge noted Bryant looked **emotional**, which made the scene feel worse than a routine cart ride from the far fields. The Bears had just made Bryant their headline free-agent add (three years, $40 million from Seattle) to lead a rebuilt secondary; he had been one of camp’s early standouts, including a pick of Caleb Williams on Saturday. Until the team speaks, severity is unknown — but the room clearly felt the weight of it.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/03/bears-s-coby-bryant-carted-off-from-practice-with-apparent-knee-injury), [Bears Wire](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/03/bears-coby-bryant-injury-update-training-camp/91149404007/), [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/coby-bryant-carted-chicago-bears-practice-leg-injury)

2. **First padded practice: trenches, 1-on-1s, and a few bright spots**
   Ben Johnson opened the morning presser “fired up” for pads, framing the day as when evaluations get real — especially along the offensive line and in the run game. The live blog from Halas Hall tracked standard camp beats: 1-on-1s, team periods, and 7-on-7 with the starters. Notables included **Colston Loveland** making an impressive **throwback catch**, **Gervarrius Owens** winning “catch of the day,” and Williams hitting **Jahdae Walker** in team work. On the line, **Braxton Jones** worked with the ones at left tackle after the Braxton Jones / Kiran Amegadjie / Jedrick Wills Jr. rotation through the non-padded week.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire — live blog](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/03/chicago-bears-training-camp-live-updates-padded-practice--live/91147758007/), [247Sports notebook](https://247sports.com/nfl/chicago-bears/article/chicago-bears-training-camp-notebook-ben-johnson-ready-to-take-practices-to-another-level-288674687/)

3. **Kyle Monangai back in full pads**
   Monangai had been out since the opening practice of camp with a soft-tissue issue Johnson described as day-to-day and likely on the **shorter** side of the recovery spectrum. He was in full pads Monday and took handoffs in warmups and drills — a relief behind D’Andre Swift. While he was out, **Salvon Ahmed** got extended second-team work (including a long run noted by Brad Biggs), giving depth backs a chance to push for roster spots now that contact is live.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/03/bears-kyle-monangai-injury-update-training-camp/91148062007/), [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/bears-rb-kyle-monangai-day-125611765.html)

4. **Johnson keeps Luther Burden hype grounded in work habits**
   Before pads, Johnson again praised second-year WR **Luther Burden III**, comparing his competitive approach to **Amon-Ra St. Brown** and stressing conditioning and daily building habits. Burden enters a larger role after the DJ Moore departure; he finished 2025 with 652 yards and two touchdowns, with most production coming late. Monday’s padded session did not reset that narrative — the coach’s message was about Year 2 standards, not a single highlight reel.
   **Sources:** [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/bears-ben-johnson-luther-burden-amon-ra-st-brown-desire-to-be-great)

5. **Nickel and depth corners in focus with Gordon still on PUP**
   With Kyler Gordon on PUP (calf), the Bears need clarity on who plays slot and who backs up outside. Monday’s rep chart included **Jaylon Jones** working at **nickel cornerback** while **Cam Lewis** slid into Bryant’s safety spot after the injury. **Terell Smith** also exited late with trainers — another name to watch on the injury report when the team comments.
   **Sources:** [Bears Wire — live blog](https://bearswire.usatoday.com/story/sports/nfl/bears/2026/08/03/chicago-bears-training-camp-live-updates-padded-practice--live/91147758007/), [ProFootballTalk](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/s-coby-bryant-carted-off-at-bears-practice)

## Roster & injuries
- **Coby Bryant (S):** Carted off Monday; apparent **left knee**; **no team diagnosis** as of this brief.
- **Terell Smith (CB):** Left practice with training staff late Monday; **unknown** issue.
- **Kyle Monangai (RB):** Soft tissue; **day-to-day**; **full pads** Monday.
- **Elijah Hicks (S):** Out of camp with a **minor shin** issue per earlier team framing; would be next up at safety if Bryant misses time.
- **Kyler Gordon (CB):** **PUP** (calf); no new return date reported today.
- **T.J. Edwards (LB):** **NFI** list (calf away from the facility); previously downplayed by Ryan Poles.
