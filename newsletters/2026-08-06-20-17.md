# Chicago Bears Brief — 2026-08-06

**Estimated read:** 5 min · **Window:** last 1 days

## TL;DR
- **Day 8** was camp’s **longest and most physical** padded practice (~2 hours, 10 minutes) — and an injury wave: **Braxton Jones**, **Montez Sweat**, **Gervarrius Owens** (carted), **Dallis Flowers**, and **Jaylon Jones** all left early.
- Early word on **Jones** is cautiously **positive** (sources / Brad Biggs: likely dodged a significant injury); **Sweat**, **Owens**, and **Flowers** have no firm diagnosis yet — Owens and Flowers looked the worst.
- **Caleb Williams** was sharper than Tuesday’s padded slog: **43-yard TD** to **Rome Odunze**, then **6-for-6 for 60 yards** and a **4-yard TD to Luther Burden** to win a two-minute drill.
- **Ben Johnson** said the team is still in the **“information collection phase”** on **Coby Bryant** and would not rubber-stamp the reported 8–10 week timeline; **Kyler Gordon** remains **status quo** on PUP rehab.
- Continuity note vs. Wednesday’s brief: Edwards’ return and the shells bounce-back still stand; today’s story is the **padded injury pile-on** and whether Jones/Sweat are short absences.

## Headlines

1. **Injury-filled Day 8 turns Halas Hall into a triage ward**
   After Wednesday’s abbreviated shells session, the Bears put the pads back on for what Johnson previewed as a heavy 11-on-11 day — and camp’s casualty list grew fast. Projected starting left tackle **Braxton Jones** exited after 1-on-1s vs. **Austin Booker**, visibly frustrated (helmet into the shrubs, left-leg stretching). Edge **Montez Sweat** pulled up in 11-on-11 and left with trainers. Later, safety **Gervarrius Owens** was **carted off** after a special-teams/punt drill leg injury, and CB **Dallis Flowers** needed help from trainers after a leg issue that Courtney Cronin described as looking “pretty seriously injured.” Slot CB **Jaylon Jones** — working with the starters with Gordon and Bryant out — also didn’t finish. Kalif Raymond was shaken up on the last play but said he was fine afterward. At 2:10, it was the longest practice of camp.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Jones/Sweat](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/06/bears-training-camp-braxton-jones-leaves-practice-injury-ben-johnson-left-tackle), [ProFootballTalk](https://www.nbcsports.com/nfl/profootballtalk/rumor-mill/news/braxton-jones-montez-sweat-left-bears-practice-early), [Sports Illustrated — Day 8](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-bears-training-camp-day-8-an-injury-filled-practice)

2. **Jones update: early optimism — but the LT battle just got murkier**
   Johnson had said before practice he hoped for **clarity at left tackle by mid-next week**. Hours later, his front-runner was inside with trainers. Post-practice sourcing was better than the optics: Sun-Times sources and the Tribune’s **Brad Biggs** reported **cautious optimism** that Jones avoided a long-term injury, with more tests ongoing. **Jedrick Wills Jr.** took first-team snaps after Jones left (Sun-Times); **Kiran Amegadjie** was also in the mix per beat notes. With **Ozzy Trapilo** still recovering from last season’s patellar tendon tear, Chicago cannot afford even a short LT absence — and Johnson reiterated they’ll cycle tackles “as long as we need to,” even into the season if required.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/06/bears-training-camp-braxton-jones-leaves-practice-injury-ben-johnson-left-tackle), [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-bears-training-camp-day-8-an-injury-filled-practice)

3. **Offense answers Tuesday’s slop with a sharp two-minute finish**
   Beat writers called Thursday up-and-down for the offense, but the closing act mattered. Williams hit Odunze for a **43-yard touchdown** (Odunze bounced off Cam Lewis and finished), then closed practice with a two-minute drive from the offense’s 35, trailing by three with 1:41 left: **6-of-6, 60 yards**, capped by a **4-yard TD to Burden** along the back of the end zone (Sun-Times). He also threw a pick to **Tyrique Stevenson**, who then **fumbled the return** back to the offense — another Stevenson highlight-and-hiccup in one play. **Cairo Santos** went a perfect **6-for-6** on field goals from 33 to 56 yards. After Press Taylor’s midweek critique of pre-snap communication, Thursday’s finish was the kind of situational win Johnson wants before the preseason opener.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Day 8 report](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/06/bears-training-camp-report-day-8-qb-caleb-williams-sharper-bryson-graham-nfl-bulls), [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/the-good-and-not-so-good-from-bears-training-camp-day-8-an-injury-filled-practice)

4. **Johnson softens Bryant timeline talk; Gordon still stuck in rehab limbo**
   Before the injury parade, Johnson addressed the secondary’s two biggest absences. On **Coby Bryant**, he said the club is still in the **“information collection phase”** on timeline and extent, expects more clarity in a few days, and pushed back on the widely reported **8–10 week** Schefter number: *“I don’t know where you guys got that… It hasn’t been said here.”* That leaves room for better *or* worse than the early report — not a clean update. On **Kyler Gordon** (PUP, calf): *“There is no update. We’re just continuing to rehab.”* Johnson did praise having **T.J. Edwards** back after Wednesday’s NFI activation, noting his communication on defense as they ramp him up.
   **Sources:** [Sports Illustrated — Bryant/Gordon](https://www.si.com/nfl/bears/onsi/bears-injury-updates-a-shred-of-hope-for-coby-bryant-but-kyler-gordon-is-status-quo), [Windy City Gridiron](https://www.windycitygridiron.com/chicago-bears-injuries/120151/chicago-bears-injury-updates-sort-of-for-coby-bryant-and-kyler-gordon), [chicagobears.com — Johnson presser](https://www.chicagobears.com/video/ben-johnson-looking-for-execution-and-growth-press-conference)

5. **Swift’s next gear: more explosives, more targets in the passing game**
   Away from the injury board, the Sun-Times’ Jason Lieser framed a quieter camp theme: Ben Johnson wants more from **D’Andre Swift** as a year-two Johnson back — more **20+ yard** runs and a larger receiving role after a career-low **34 catches** in 2025. Swift (1,087 rush yards, 4.9 YPC last year) said there’s “a lot more out there,” and Johnson wants to “challenge him” as a receiver without losing the north-south identity Eric Bieniemy drilled in. With Odunze, Burden, Loveland, Kmet, Raymond, and rookie Zavion Thomas already on the menu, the point is contingency: more versatile pieces so the offense stays overwhelming when injuries or elite defenses thin the options — a theme that hit differently on a day the roster thinned in real time.
   **Sources:** [Chicago Sun-Times — Swift](https://chicago.suntimes.com/bears/2026/08/06/expanding-bears-rb-dandre-swifts-role-all-about-creating-overwhelming-set-of-options-for-coach-ben-johnson)
